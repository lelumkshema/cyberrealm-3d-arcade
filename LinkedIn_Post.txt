🚀 Excited to announce CyberRealm 3D — an open-source 3D WebGL Vehicle Combat & Procedural Audio Engine Architecture! 🎮⚡

I have been working on building a lightweight, zero-asset 3D simulation framework inside client browsers using Three.js, React, Web Audio API, and Speech Synthesis AI.

✨ Key Innovations & Features:
🔹 Procedural 3D WebGL Engine: Real-time Three.js rendering with 3rd-person chase camera, dynamic lighting & atmospheric fog.
🔹 Homing Missile Kinematics & EMP Blast: Vector-calculated missile trajectories with particle smoke trails and expanding plasma shockwaves.
🔹 360° Interactive 3D Armory Garage: Real-time vehicle customization, paint coat swapping, and neon underglow shaders.
🔹 Zero-Asset Procedural Audio & Voice AI: Web Audio API sound synthesis and real-time military briefing voice announcements.

📊 Experimental Evaluation Results:
✅ Consistent 60 FPS Frame Rate across standard devices
✅ 1.42s Production Compilation (Vite 6)
✅ 216 KB Gzip Bundle Size (Zero external 3D mesh overhead!)
